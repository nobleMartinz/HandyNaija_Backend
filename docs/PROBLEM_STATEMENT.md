# HANDYNAIJA Problem Statement

**Version 1.0 | October 2026**

---

## The Problem: Finding Local Services in Nigeria is Broken

### Current Reality
People in Nigeria depend on **personal recommendations, WhatsApp groups, friends and family, social media, or informal contacts** to find local professionals like electricians, plumbers, mechanics, cleaners, tutors, and technicians.

This informal approach creates **systemic problems** for both customers and service providers:

---

## Customer Pain Points

| Problem | Impact |
|---------|--------|
| **Difficulty finding trustworthy providers** | No verified directory; rely on word-of-mouth which is limited and biased |
| **Limited provider information** | Can't compare experience, services, or qualifications before hiring |
| **Unclear pricing** | No standard rates; quotes vary wildly; no way to compare beforehand |
| **Poor communication & missed appointments** | Phone-tag, no-shows, no structured scheduling or reminders |
| **No access to reviews** | Can't learn from others' experiences; no accountability for quality |
| **Hard to find nearby professionals** | Geographic search is manual; "nearby" means "who your neighbor knows" |
| **No structured service requests** | Job details lost in chat; scope creep; disputes over what was agreed |

---

## Provider Pain Points

| Problem | Impact |
|---------|--------|
| **Limited digital presence** | No professional profile to showcase skills, experience, portfolio |
| **Hard to reach relevant customers** | Rely on referrals; can't be discovered by people actively searching |
| **Unstructured job requests** | Vague WhatsApp messages; no clear scope, location, or timeline |
| **Difficulty managing availability** | No calendar system; double-bookings; customers assume 24/7 availability |
| **Hard to manage jobs** | No pipeline view; can't track pending/active/completed jobs |
| **Fragmented communication** | Switch between calls, WhatsApp, SMS; lose context |
| **Can't demonstrate reputation** | No verified reviews; new customers have no trust signals |

---

## Marketplace-Level Problems

| Problem | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Low provider supply** | Not enough providers in specific categories/locations initially |
| **Fake provider profiles** | False/misleading information erodes trust |
| **Poor service quality** | Platform doesn't guarantee workmanship outcomes |
| **Off-platform communication** | Users move to WhatsApp/calls; platform loses visibility & safety |
| **No-shows & cancellations** | Appointments not fulfilled; no accountability mechanisms |
| **Marketplace imbalance** | Supply/demand mismatch across services and locations |
| **Privacy exposure** | Customer addresses/contact info unnecessarily exposed |

---

## How HANDYNAIJA Solves This

### For Customers
✅ **Searchable directory** of verified providers by service, location, rating  
✅ **Rich provider profiles** with experience, services, availability, reviews  
✅ **Structured service requests** with clear scope, location, preferred time  
✅ **Real-time status tracking** (Pending → Accepted → Scheduled → In Progress → Completed)  
✅ **In-platform messaging** tied to specific requests  
✅ **Verified reviews** only after completed services  
✅ **Provider verification badges** for trust signals  

### For Providers
✅ **Professional profile** showcasing business, experience, services, areas  
✅ **Service listings** tied to categories for discoverability  
✅ **Availability management** (weekly schedule + exceptions)  
✅ **Structured incoming requests** with all details upfront  
✅ **Accept/Reject/Reschedule** workflow with notifications  
✅ **Job pipeline dashboard** (New → Upcoming → Active → Completed)  
✅ **Review collection** builds reputation automatically  

### For the Marketplace
✅ **Admin moderation** for users, providers, reviews, reports  
✅ **Category management** keeps marketplace organized  
✅ **Audit logging** for security-sensitive actions  
✅ **Verification workflow** establishes trust tiers  
✅ **Reporting system** for fraud, abuse, safety issues  

---

## The Core Marketplace Loop

```
DISCOVER → EVALUATE → REQUEST → COMMUNICATE → COMPLETE → REVIEW
```

**Customer finds provider** → **Evaluates profile** → **Sends structured request** → 
**Provider responds** → **Both communicate in-platform** → **Service completed** → 
**Customer leaves verified review** → **Provider reputation grows** → **More customers discover**

---

## Why This Matters for Nigeria

- **Informal economy dominance**: 65%+ of workforce in informal sector; no digital infrastructure
- **Trust deficit**: High fraud risk in service transactions; no recourse mechanisms
- **Mobile-first population**: 100M+ internet users; web app accessible without app store
- **Local context**: State/City/Area hierarchy matches Nigerian addressing
- **Economic empowerment**: Gives skilled professionals access to digital customer base

---

## Success Vision

A **trusted digital marketplace** where:
- Customers find the right professional **without relying only on personal referrals**
- Providers get discovered by customers **actively looking for their service**
- The platform starts with **focused categories in one geographic market**
- Expands as **usage and operational experience grow**
